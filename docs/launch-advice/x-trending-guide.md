# How to Get Your Launch Trending on X — Full Guide

Source: [@0xfJuan on X](https://x.com/0xfJuan), July 28, 2026. Author runs Ark Media, a growth/PR shop for tech launches; claims 6M+ impressions driven for clients in 2 weeks, one launch hitting 2.4M impressions in 24 hours.

## How trending actually works

X samples a post to a small group of followers first, watches engagement in that window, and only expands reach if the sample reacts. Three factors decide advancement:

1. **Speed** — 50 real interactions in 15 minutes beats 1,000 spread over 3 days. Fast engagement reads as "an event."
2. **Signal weight** — likes are cheap and count for little; replies, reposts, and bookmarks are expensive signals the algorithm trusts more.
3. **Breadth** — trending isn't one post doing big numbers, it's *many accounts* posting about the same thing in the same few hours. That's when the algorithm treats it as a "subject" and pushes it to people who follow no one involved.

## Stage 1 — Research (build a swipe file)

- Get X data access (e.g., twitterapi.io — cheap, no approval process).
- Compile 50+ accounts talking to your target audience: direct competitors, adjacent products, everyone in your category who launched in the last 6 months.
- Use Claude Code to pull their posts, filter for above-average performers, and extract patterns: hook construction, claim type, image vs. video, the ask.
- Output: a ranked swipe file with notes on *why* each post worked.
- Rule: study the swipe file for **patterns**, not sentences — write your own copy.

## Stage 2 — Hook (video + first line + lead magnet)

- **Video**: 60–90 seconds. The most impressive thing the product does must be visible in the first 5 seconds, understandable muted. Use a framing/angle your swipe file shows nobody in your category has used yet.
- **First line**: a claim, not a description — claims start arguments. Best claims declare the old way dead, plant a "first of its kind" flag, or attack something the market resents. Push to the edge of believable — test by seeing if a non-technical person can repeat it back over dinner.
- **Lead magnet**: offer something genuinely valuable in the first reply (playbook, template, early access) in exchange for a comment. Comments are an expensive signal, so a good gate also farms algorithm trust during the judgment window.

## Stage 3 — Attention (creator seeding, timed)

Line up three creator tiers before launch day:
- **Big accounts** — raw reach.
- **Mid-size niche experts** — trust; their followers are real buyers.
- **Small accounts (hundreds)** — makes the conversation look like it's coming from everywhere, which is what the algorithm is actually counting.

Brief everyone in advance: exact posting minute, link, claim, several angles to avoid a uniform "press release" look. Everyone writes in their own voice.

**Timing**: big accounts + experts post within hour one (the algorithm's decision window); small accounts roll out in waves across the next three hours. Target 200+ posts across the first 4 hours — this reads as organic discovery, not a campaign.

## Stage 4 — Conversion (the first 48 hours)

- Reply to every comment — each reply restarts the conversation under the post.
- DM everyone who shared/quoted the post, same day.
- Fast-deliver the lead magnet with a follow-up question ("what are you building?") to convert downloaders into conversations.
- Post plain-number milestones (signups, views, waitlist size) to give onlookers a story to track.
- Reply publicly to late-arriving big accounts.
- Cut new content from what's working (video clips, screenshots, founder notes).
- For 48 hours, be one story — don't post anything else.
- After the spike: stay visible daily. Most buyers convert weeks later, and they buy from whoever they've seen most consistently since.

## TL;DR

1. **Research**: Claude-built swipe file from 50+ niche accounts' top posts.
2. **Hook**: 60–90s video (payoff in first 5 sec), a checkable claim as the first line, comment-gated lead magnet in reply 1.
3. **Attention**: big + mid + small creators, briefed and timed in waves, 200+ posts in 4 hours.
4. **Conversion**: reply to everything, DM sharers, fast lead-magnet delivery + a question, milestone posts for 48h, then stay visible long-term.
