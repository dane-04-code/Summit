## Triage Labels

- `Confirmed` - backed by code, tests, docs, or explicit decision.
- `Blocker` - prevents MVP or reliable testing.
- `Decision` - needs Dane to choose a direction.
- `Candidate` - potentially useful but not accepted.
- `Backlog` - good later, not MVP.
- `Stale` - likely outdated but needs review before removal.
