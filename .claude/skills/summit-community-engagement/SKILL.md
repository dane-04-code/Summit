# Summit Community Engagement

Draft only. Reddit and Discord communities burn accounts (and the product's reputation) that post like a bot; private DMs sent at any automated scale read as spam. Every output here is a draft for Dane to personally review and send.

## Rules

- Write as a person sharing something they built, not a company announcing a product. Disclose Summit ownership plainly whenever it's directly relevant or asked — no undisclosed astroturfing.
- Reddit/Discord: reply to or contribute in genuine context (a real thread, a real question) rather than posting cold. Link proof (the device-test sequence) over a feature list.
- DMs: one-to-one, small scale, only to people who show a real, specific fit (e.g., mentions running Hermes/OpenClaw, asks a related question). Never mass-DM or use a template verbatim across multiple people.
- Every claim used must trace to summit-release-evidence's proven set, same as any other external copy.

## Output

Return the draft, the specific target (thread/user/channel) and why they're a fit, and the claim sources used. Never submit or send on the user's behalf.
