# Summit Distribution

Execute the channel plan summit-growth-command sets, one channel at a time. Read `docs/PUBLISHING_COPY.md` and `docs/GO_TO_MARKET_PLAN.md`; use `product-brief.md` for audience/positioning.

## Audience discipline

The audience is technical self-hosters who already run Hermes — not general AI-chat users. Reject tactics built for that wrong audience: broad-keyword ASO ("AI chat app"), generic AI-productivity SEO, or paid acquisition funnels. Every channel choice should assume the reader already knows what a self-hosted agent is.

## Channel notes

- **r/openclaw / Hermes Discord** — Post as a community member sharing a tool, not a corporate announcement. Link the proof (device-test sequence), not a feature list.
- **App Store listing/ASO** — Keywords should target "self-hosted agent," "Hermes," "agent client" over generic chatbot terms. Screenshots show the real conversation surface, not marketing mockups.
- **Product Hunt** — Time it after community channels show organic pull (per summit-growth-command); a cold launch there wastes the one shot at that audience.

## Non-negotiables

- Every claim used in channel copy must trace to summit-release-evidence's proven set. Do not originate new claims here — request copy from summit-launch-copy or verify against the ledger directly.
- No paid spend on any channel until an organic signal (repeat engagement, inbound pairing requests) validates it.
- One job per channel: recruit, explain, or prove. Don't combine goals into a single scattershot post.

## Output

Return, per channel: goal (recruit/explain/prove), draft or brief, claim sources used, timing dependency, and owner. Flag any request that would require an unproven claim.
